How it works

The calculator determines the base arena points from your rating and then applies the appropriate multiplier for each bracket. Your weekly arena points are determined by whichever eligible bracket gives you the highest number of points. The application is intended for The Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary servers.

Note: You must play at least 10 games in a bracket to be eligible for points from that bracket.
Base formula:

p = ((1651.94 − 475) / (1 + 2,500,000 × 2.71828−0.009r) + 475) × 1.5
  • 2v2: 76% of base points
  • 3v3: 88% of base points
  • 5v5: 100% of base points
